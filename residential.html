The Process

What We Clean & How

Every residential clean follows our thorough 6-point process, covering every component of your system.

🌬️

Filters

Filters are removed, washed and dried. Blocked filters are the #1 cause of poor performance and contaminated air.

🧊

Evaporator Coils

High-pressure foam cleaning of evaporator coils removes dust, mould and biofilm that accumulate over time.

💨

Fan Blower Wheel

The fan/blower is cleaned to remove the thick dust and mould that builds up and gets blown into your room.

💧

Drain Pan & Pipe

The condensate drain pan and pipe are cleared and treated to prevent mould growth and water overflow issues.

🧪

Sanitise & Treat

We apply an antibacterial and anti-mould treatment to all internal surfaces, eliminating odour-causing organisms.

✅

Test & Report

The unit is tested post-clean. You receive a simple before/after report confirming the work done.
