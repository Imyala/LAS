Our Story

Brisbane's Trusted Aircon Cleaning Specialists

LAS was founded by Luke with a simple mission: provide the kind of honest, thorough aircon cleaning service that he couldn't find anywhere else. After years working in the HVAC industry, Luke saw first-hand how many systems were being serviced with a quick surface clean and called "done" — leaving homeowners and businesses paying for something that barely scratched the surface of what their system needed.

Starting with just a van and a commitment to doing the job properly, LAS has grown to serve hundreds of residential and commercial customers across Brisbane and surrounding regions. Our reputation is built on doing what we say, showing up when we say, and leaving every system genuinely cleaner than we found it.

Today, LAS is the go-to name for aircon cleaning in Brisbane — trusted by homeowners, property managers, businesses and industrial operators who know that when LAS has been, the job has truly been done right.
