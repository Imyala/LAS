About Cold Circuit

An owner-operated aircon cleaning business built on honesty, thoroughness and genuinely clean, healthy air.

Our Story

One Trusted Person, On Every Job

Cold Circuit was started with a simple frustration: too many aircon "cleans" are a quick wipe of the filter and a call to say it's done — leaving people paying for something that barely scratches the surface of what their system needs.

So Cold Circuit does it differently. It's a deliberately small, owner-operated business — which means the person you book is the person who turns up, does the work, and stands behind it. No subcontractors, no rushed jobs, no surprises. Every system is opened up, properly deep-cleaned and left running better than we found it.

From homes across Logan and Brisbane to businesses down on the Gold Coast, the goal is always the same: cleaner air, a healthier system, and a job you'd happily recommend to a mate.
