FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I have my aircon cleaned? ▼
We recommend residential units are cleaned at least once a year, ideally before summer. Commercial and high-use systems benefit from cleaning every 6 months. Industrial systems may require quarterly maintenance depending on usage.
How long does an aircon clean take? ▼
A standard residential split system clean takes 45–75 minutes. Ducted systems or multiple units take longer. Commercial and industrial jobs are quoted on-site based on system size and complexity.
Is your cleaning process safe for my family and pets? ▼
Yes. We use eco-friendly, biodegradable cleaning agents that are completely safe for humans, children and pets once the unit is running. We'll always advise you of any specific precautions before we begin.
Do you service all brands of air conditioners? ▼
Yes — we service all major brands including Daikin, Mitsubishi Electric, Fujitsu, Panasonic, LG, Samsung, Hitachi, Toshiba, Carrier, and more. Residential, commercial and industrial models all welcomed.
Will a clean improve my aircon's efficiency? ▼
Absolutely. A dirty system works harder to reach the set temperature, consuming significantly more energy. Many customers report a noticeable reduction in their electricity bill after a professional clean.
Do you offer maintenance contracts for businesses? ▼
Yes! We offer flexible preventive maintenance contracts for commercial and industrial clients, including scheduled visits, priority booking and discounted rates. Contact us to discuss a plan that suits your needs.
