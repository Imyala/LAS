The Process

How We Clean Your Aircon

Here's exactly what happens during a service — a thorough, methodical clean that leaves your system healthy and running at its best.

1

Inspect & Assess

We check over your system, note its condition and take baseline readings so we know exactly what we're working with.

2

Dismantle & Protect

The unit is carefully opened up and the surrounding area is covered and protected before any cleaning begins.

3

Deep Clean

We wash the coils, filters, fan/blower and drain — clearing the dust, mould and biofilm that build up inside the unit.

4

Sanitise & Treat

An antibacterial, anti-mould treatment is applied to internal surfaces to kill odour-causing organisms and leave the air fresh.

5

Test & Take Readings

We reassemble, run the system and record key readings — airflow, supply and return air temperatures (plus water flow and temps on applicable systems) — to confirm it's performing as it should.

Did You Know?

The dark, damp inside of an aircon is an ideal home for microbes. One study identified nearly 100 species of bacteria and 60+ fungi living inside home AC units — a common trigger for allergies and asthma. Regular cleaning keeps your air healthy.
