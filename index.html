The Process

How Our Aircon Clean Works

Our proven 5-step process ensures your system is thoroughly cleaned, sanitised and performing at its best.

1

Book & Confirm

Call or fill in our online form. We'll confirm your appointment and provide an upfront quote at a time that suits you.

2

Arrive & Inspect

Our technician arrives on time, assesses your system and explains the work to be completed before starting anything.

3

Deep Clean

We dismantle the unit, high-pressure clean coils, sanitise filters, clear drains and treat for mould and bacteria.

4

Test & Verify

The unit is reassembled and tested to confirm it's operating correctly and efficiently.

5

Report & Depart

You receive a service report with photos, work completed and any recommendations. We leave your home clean.

❄️

Did You Know?

A dirty aircon can harbour up to 2 million colony-forming units of bacteria per unit — causing allergies, asthma flare-ups and even illness. Regular cleaning is essential for your family's health.
